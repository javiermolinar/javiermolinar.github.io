# Javi Molina
### An Insider View of a Distributed World

I’m a Senior Backend Engineer at **Grafana Labs** and a core maintainer of **Grafana Tempo**, working on the infrastructure that makes the internet observable.

On this blog, I explore the technical problems of systems architecture, the ethics of privacy, and engineering philosophy. I work to build great systems, but I live to enjoy the world beyond them.
