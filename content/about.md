# Javi Molina
### An Insider View of a Distributed World

I’m a Senior Backend Engineer at **Grafana Labs** and a core maintainer of **Grafana Tempo**, working on the infrastructure that makes the internet observable.

I named this site **Kagi** (the Japanese word for **key / 鍵**) because I believe that as AI takes over the "boilerplate" of coding, the real value of an engineer lies in finding the keys to **reasoning** and **reliability**.

On this blog, I explore the deep technical problems of systems architecture, the ethics of privacy, and engineering philosophy. I work to build great systems, but I live to enjoy the world beyond them.
